About Kitchen Counter

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19

Kitchen Counter is a small publication that compares kitchen appliances by the numbers on their labels. We cover air fryers and meat thermometers — five products, each transcribed from dated official Amazon listing snapshots. We attribute specs to the seller; we do not publish independent product tests.

Who writes this

No individual author is named on this site. That is a deficit, not a style choice: search guidance asks who created a page and what first-hand knowledge they bring, and this desk cannot answer either question yet. Naming an editor with a real author note is recorded as proposal SG-19 and open question Q-05. Until an owner decides, read the pages as a sourced summary of listings, not as a person's tested opinion.

How we verify

Product details are transcribed from dated official listing snapshots, with source links in the Source register. A seller's claim is not an independent test. If a value is unconfirmed, it stays blank and is flagged; if a listing contradicts itself, both statements stay visible. Open items live on Flagged for Review.

How we disclose

The site owner supplied tracking ID shopnow047c-20 for affiliate use. Account assignment and eligibility, and the supplied short links' compliance, have not been verified. The disclosure appears above the fold and in the footer; outbound product links are marked rel="sponsored". See How we disclose and FLG-006/009.

How we measure

Privacy-preserving: no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal data. Four events are counted locally (page_view, outbound_click, article_read, chooser_complete); reader_signup is reserved for a confirmed email-provider response and the static site has no such provider. The campaign ledger stays empty until authorized first-party reports exist. We do not present conversion scores or revenue forecasts. See Analytics & privacy.

How we decide what to publish next

The Direction page holds suggestions, open questions, and a path chooser. Nothing is implemented until a path is chosen there.
