What the gaps mean

Where a cell reads “Not published”, the listing simply does not state that number. It is not a zero and it is not a bad sign — it is a question to ask the seller, or a reason to look at a different listing. Measuring the space on your own counter is still the only way to know whether any of these will fit; the listed dimensions do not include the clearance a running appliance needs.

A quart is not a portion. The listed capacity tells you how big the cooking container is. It does not tell you how much food comes out, and no number on this page was produced by cooking anything. See the capacity checklist before you let a number decide for you.
