Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19 · 6 min read · Specs from official listings

"Multi-function" is the most abused phrase in kitchen retail. The Emeril 26QT lists 24 presets (title) or 25 (About this item) — we flag the conflict instead of repeating either number as fact. Ask these six questions of any multi-function listing.

The six questions

  1. Which functions will I actually use weekly? The Emeril covers air fry, bake, broil, dehydrate, roast, slow cook, and more. If you will only air-fry, a $69.99 single basket beats a $149.99 oven.
  2. Does it fit my counter depth? Emeril: 16.9 in deep. Measure yours first.
  3. How many parts do I wash? Emeril: six accessories. Chefman 8 Qt: basket + tray. Count them.
  4. What does the warranty actually say? Chefman: 1-year. CHEF iQ: 2-year assurance (About) vs 3 Year (Item details) — flagged. Read both places.
  5. Does the listing agree with itself? Preset count, battery life, warranty — check title vs About vs Product Information. Two of our five listings do not agree.
  6. Is there a newer model? The Chefman 8 Qt page lists a newer 6-Qt model; the ThermoMaven page lists a newer orange model. Check before you buy.
Multi-function is a trade, not a gift: you gain versatility and lose simplicity. The right answer depends on which functions you will use — not on how many the listing counts.
Should I buy the multi-function model to save counter space?

Only if you will retire the appliances it replaces. The Emeril replaces a toaster oven and a dehydrator; it does not replace a stove. Count the appliances it truly replaces.
