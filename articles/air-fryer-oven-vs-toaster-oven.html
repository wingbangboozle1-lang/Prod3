Air-fryer oven footprint test: dimensions and clearance

Kitchen Counter · Editorial correction 2026-11-19 · Listing claims are not product tests

This is a fit-checking guide, not a head-to-head toaster-oven test: we have not selected or measured a toaster-oven model. The Emeril Lagasse 26QT listing is one oven-style example. Use the same checks with any countertop appliance.

Record the exterior dimensions

The Emeril listing gives 16.9 × 15.8 × 14.8 in (D×W×H). Compare all three measurements with the intended spot, including nearby walls, cabinets, outlets, and the door or controls as relevant. The listing dimensions are not a clearance instruction; consult the maker's manual for operating space requirements.

Check the usable interior separately

The listing states a 26-quart capacity. That number does not tell you the usable dimensions for a particular pan or food. Check interior measurements and the maker's fit guidance for the item you expect to cook; do not infer that a 6-lb chicken, 12-inch pizza, or any other specific item will fit unless the official source states it.

Inspect the listing's claims and conflicts

The Emeril product page gives 24 presets in the title and 25 in “About this item.” We record both, rather than choosing one (FLG-002). Before relying on any named function or preset, check the actual product manual and controls.

Count included components, then read care instructions

The listing names a rack, bake pan, drip tray, crisper basket, rotisserie spit, and fetch tool. A list of included components is not a cleanup test; see the cleaning and counter-space guide and follow the manual for each part.

A useful footprint check: compare exterior depth, width, and height; verify required clearance; then confirm that the usable interior fits your actual cookware or food. We do not declare a toaster oven or air-fryer oven the better buy without a matched comparison.
Does this establish that an air-fryer oven is better than a toaster oven?

No. No toaster-oven model is compared here, and no cooking or toast performance was tested. This page is a dimensional checklist only.

Product source: Emeril Lagasse 26QT official listing (seller-stated dimensions, capacity, components, and preset-count conflict).
