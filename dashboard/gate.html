Compliance gate: fourteen checks and where the human steps in

Fourteen checks against Amazon's Operating Agreement and Program Policies, FTC disclosure guidance and Google publishing policy, each marked as automated or human-gated.

Automate what can be automated, and put a gate in front of what cannot. Each check below names the obligation, the primary source it comes from, whether a machine enforces it, and what a machine cannot enforce.

What the build refuses to ship

  • A page without the disclosure strip.
  • An Amazon link without rel="sponsored".
  • A currency symbol, a price pattern, a promotional discount phrase, or a delivery estimate.
  • A duplicate title or meta description.
  • A page with zero or more than one <h1>.
  • An internal link that does not resolve.
