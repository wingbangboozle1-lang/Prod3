About Kitchen Counter

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19

Kitchen Counter is a small publication that compares kitchen appliances by the numbers on their labels. We cover air fryers and meat thermometers — five products, each verified line-by-line against its official Amazon listing.

How we verify

Every spec, rating, price, and rank is read from the official listing page and linked to it. If a value cannot be confirmed, it is left blank and flagged. If a listing contradicts itself, both numbers are published and flagged — never silently resolved. The full register is on the Source register page; open items live on Flagged for Review.

How we disclose

We are Amazon Associates (tracking ID shopnow047c-20). The disclosure sits above the fold on every page and repeats in the footer. Every outbound product link is marked rel="sponsored". See How we disclose.

How we measure

Privacy-preserving: no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal data. Five events are counted locally (page_view, outbound_click, article_read, chooser_complete, reader_signup). The campaign ledger starts empty and only ever records numbers from authorized first-party reports. We do not present conversion scores or revenue forecasts.

How we decide what to publish next

The Direction page holds suggestions, open questions, and a path chooser. Nothing is implemented until a path is chosen there.
