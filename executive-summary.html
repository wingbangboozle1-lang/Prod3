Executive summary · decision brief

Kitchen Counter: a buyer-first kitchen publication

Status reviewed 2026-11-20 · Snapshot records dated 2026-11-19 · Tracking ID shopnow047c-20

Bottom line: the publication is built and its repository release gates pass. It presents five products, buyer guides, sourcing and disclosures. The intended products behind all five supplied short links remain unconfirmed; the Associates account workflow, aggregate analytics, Search Console data and conversion reports are not connected. Recommendation—not a recorded owner decision: clear the account/link checks before scaling, then keep publishing and use measured query and first-party report data to guide the next topics.

What is ready

The front page leads with products and buyer questions, not the verification process. Visitors can browse two categories, compare five listings, use a three-question chooser, read a buying guide and nine question-shaped articles (eight evergreen guides plus the dated Thanksgiving temperature plan), use three decision tools, and subscribe to the RSS feed in their own reader. The source register, citation log, suggestions, open questions, analytics status and flagged findings remain reachable from the footer and navigation.

  • The Associates disclosure appears above the first heading and again in each page footer; every Amazon link is marked rel="sponsored".
  • Seller-listed specifications are presented as seller claims, not independent test results. Listing conflicts are kept visible.
  • No prices or customer ratings are presented on the site. Amazon program policies are linked in the source register.
  • The five event names are defined. Four event counters are local to each visitor's browser; signup is reserved for a provider-confirmed response. No event endpoint or shared totals are configured.
  • The campaign ledger is empty by design. No click, order, commission, conversion score, or revenue forecast is claimed.

The three decisions that actually gate revenue

  1. Are the links tagged? Amazon's Program Policies require an Associates ID or tag as a URL parameter on every link to an Amazon site. The five supplied link.amazon short links carry no visible tag in the URL a reader receives. One signed-in click per product in SiteStripe settles it (DT-01). This is not a claim that the links are invalid — it is an unresolved status.
  2. Is anything measured? There is no analytics endpoint and no Search Console property, so the site cannot say which page or product works. Both are free and take minutes, not days (DT-03, DT-07).
  3. Can the site say anything first-hand? Every figure on every page is a seller statement. Two purchased units would let it publish its own measurements and stop qualifying every sentence (DT-05).

Fix only the first one and the site can earn. Fix the first two and it can learn. All three are on the decision desk with their cost and the source each rests on.

Questions to answer before choosing a path

  1. Which product did the owner intend for each submitted link? Confirm in the Associates account; record the intended listing separately from the observed redirect.
  2. Is the tracking ID enrolled and eligible, and do the links report correctly? Check Associates Central and test one link per product before broader use.
  3. What audience and query data exists? Verify Search Console, submit the sitemap, then review actual impressions and queries; none are present in this repository.
  4. What analytics host, retention, and privacy notice should be used? GitHub Pages is static and cannot receive the current events; no endpoint, email provider, or site-wide counts are configured.
  5. Who will own the publishing cadence, brand, and testing budget? The repo has proposals, not an approved owner, domain, schedule, or product-testing plan.
  6. What first-party campaign reports are available? Add a dated ledger row only when eligible sessions, clicks, or reported orders are obtained from an authorized report.

Discoveries and decision ownership are tracked in the suggestions and path chooser and open-question register. No one path has been marked chosen.
