Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19 · 6 min read · Specs from official listings

The air fryer market splits into two shapes: basket fryers that cook in a drawer, and oven-style units that air-fry inside a countertop oven. Our verified set has both — the Chefman 6 Qt dual basket and the Emeril Lagasse 26QT oven combo. Here is what to compare, in order.

1. Capacity per inch of depth

The Chefman dual basket holds 6 quarts in 13.38 inches of depth (0.45 qt/in). The Emeril oven holds 26 quarts in 16.9 inches (1.54 qt/in). If counter depth is your constraint, the oven combo wins by a factor of three. If you cook for two, the basket is plenty.

2. Temperature range

Chefman dual: 200–450°F. Emeril oven: 75–500°F. The oven's low end (75°F) covers dehydrating and proofing; the basket's 200°F floor does not. If you only fry and roast, the floor rarely matters.

3. Cooking two things at once

Only the dual basket has two independent baskets with Sync Finish. The oven cooks one load at a time but fits a whole chicken or a 12-inch pizza — the basket cannot.

4. Price

The oven costs roughly double and replaces several appliances; the basket does one job well.

Rule of thumb: two people, two foods, tight counter → dual basket. Family, whole roasts, roomy counter → oven combo. Full numbers in the comparison.
Is an oven-style air fryer actually faster?

Neither listing publishes a timed comparison, so we do not claim one. Wattage is identical (1700W); speed depends on load size and temperature, not shape.

Which is easier to clean?

The Chefman baskets are nonstick and dishwasher-safe (listing). The Emeril includes a drip tray and crisper basket. See cleanup and counter-space trade-offs.
