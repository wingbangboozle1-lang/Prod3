Best for

Two-part dinners for two or three people, where the protein and the side need different times and you want them to land together.

Not for

A whole chicken, a 12 in pizza, or anyone with less than about 14 in of counter depth.

What the listing says

  • Two independent 3 qt baskets with a combined 6 qt capacity, and Sync Finish to align the two timers.
  • 13.38 in deep by 11.81 in wide by 10.63 in tall; black and grey; metal and plastic construction.
  • Features listed: Hi-Fry, a 2-minute button, automatic shut-off and a mute option.
  • At capture the listing showed 4.2 out of 5 stars from 4,876 ratings and an Amazon's Choice badge.

Where it is weak

The capacity is stated twice and the two do not agree: 6 quarts in the title, 6 liters in the specification field. Ambiguity in the headline number of a listing is worth knowing about before you buy, not after.

The fit test

If the largest single item you cook is a whole bird, this is the wrong machine. If you cook two things at once most nights, it is the right one.

Quick read

  • Two independent baskets, so a protein and a side can cook at once instead of in sequence.
  • Sync Finish is the feature that matters for two-basket cooking: it lines up the two timers to end together.
  • The footprint, 13.38 in deep by 11.81 in wide, is the number to measure your counter against before anything else.

Watch out for

  • The Capacity field says 6 liters while the title says 6 quarts. 6 US quarts is about 5.68 litres, so the field is not a straight conversion. Both are printed on the listing; neither is corrected here (F-01).
  • Two 3 qt baskets do not behave like one 6 qt basket. A 6 qt single-basket machine holds a whole chicken; two 3 qt drawers hold pieces.
  • The listing puts a paid Asurion protection plan next to the buy box. It is an add-on, not part of the product.
