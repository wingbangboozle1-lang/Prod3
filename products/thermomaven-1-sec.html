Who this one fits

Consider it if you want a fast handheld spot-check for grills and roasts, and you are comfortable treating the accuracy numbers as claims to verify against the included certificate — with the guide above open.

Look elsewhere if you want leave-in monitoring that tracks a cook without you standing over the grill — that is the CHEF iQ Sense's job — or if the missing probe length matters for the roasts you actually make.
