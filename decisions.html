4. Choose a path

Three coherent routes. Each one is costed, each one says what it delays, and none of them requires a decision you cannot reverse. Naming a path does not change the site by itself — it goes into the decision log first.

Make it earn

Fix the links and the measurement first, then write. Nothing new is published until a click can be counted.

Includes

  • DT-01: one signed-in Special Link test per product
  • DT-07: choose and stand up the analytics endpoint
  • DT-03: Search Console verified, sitemap submitted
  • Then one guide, chosen from search data

Delays

  • The next guide
  • Any brand or domain change

Cost: One session for the link test, one focused day for the endpoint

Best if: The goal is revenue in the next ninety days.

Make it a publication

Buy the two units, publish real measurements, and let first-hand testing carry the writing.

Delays

  • Costs money before it earns any

Best if: The goal is a site that ranks and is worth reading in a year.

Narrow and harden
