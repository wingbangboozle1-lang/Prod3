F-01 high Specification conflict Chefman 6 Qt Dual Basket

The listing's Capacity field reads "6 liters" while the title and bullets read "6-quart" with two 3-quart baskets. 6 US quarts is about 5.68 litres, so the field is not a conversion of the title.

Why it matters. A buyer sizing a machine against a litre-based recipe or a litre-labelled competitor is working from a number that does not match the page's own headline.

What was done. Both readings are published verbatim and the conflict is named on the product page. Not resolved by inference.

Status. open — needs a measurement or a retailer clarification
