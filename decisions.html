Two things are wrong on this site right now. Both are published here rather than quietly corrected, because a fix nobody can check is just a different story.

Register defect: reproduced, then resolved the same day. Two different identifiers name one proposal: SG-20 and SG-21 both read “Pick one product as the site's spine and let the other four support it”. The same failure — one ID, two meanings — was fixed for SG-01..SG-08 in an earlier audit, so this is a recurrence, not a one-off. A new gate rule (D20) now fails the build if two live proposals ever share a title again. What the owner may still want to confirm: that no proposal was displaced by the duplicate — only the owner knows whether a second, different idea was ever meant to occupy SG-21.

4. Choose a path

Three coherent routes. Each one is costed, each one says what it delays, and none of them requires a decision you cannot reverse. Naming a path does not change the site by itself — it goes into the decision log first.

Path A

Make it earn

Fix the links and the measurement first, then write. Nothing new is published until a click can be counted.

Includes

  • DT-01: one signed-in Special Link test per product
  • DT-07: choose and stand up the analytics endpoint
  • DT-03: Search Console verified, sitemap submitted
  • Then one guide, chosen from search data

Delays

  • Guide nine
  • Any brand or domain change

Cost: One session for the link test, one focused day for the endpoint

Best if: The goal is revenue in the next ninety days.

Path B

Make it a publication

Buy the two units, publish real measurements, and let first-hand testing carry the writing.

Includes

  • DT-05: one air fryer and one thermometer purchased
  • Test notes and measured temperatures published as method
  • A named author with a stated basis for the claim
  • Cadence fixed at one guide a month

Delays

  • Nothing compliance-critical
  • Costs money before it earns any

Cost: Purchase price of two units, plus a recurring writing commitment

Best if: The goal is a site that ranks and is worth reading in a year.

Path C

Narrow and harden

Keep air fryers, retire the thermometer shelf, and resolve every owner-only question before adding anything.

Includes

  • DT-06: air fryers as the single beat
  • DT-09: settle the URL before promoting it
  • Clear the owner-only question rows (Q-02, Q-03, Q-04, Q-18, Q-19)
  • No new content until the desk is empty

Delays

  • Any growth work
  • The thermometer guides lose their home

Cost: Mostly time, and one irreversible-ish pruning decision

Best if: The site is a side project that must stay small and correct.
