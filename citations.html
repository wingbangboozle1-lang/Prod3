The source register lists the pages we read. This log is the other half: one row per figure the site renders, naming the observation, its date, and the official page it came from. If a figure has no row here, it does not belong on the site.

The rule, stated plainly. A value is published only if it traces to (a) a dated fetch of the official listing, (b) a dated fetch of an official policy page, or (c) arithmetic over (a) that shows its inputs. Everything else is left blank and published on Flagged for Review. No price or availability value is rendered, so none appears here.
