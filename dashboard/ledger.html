Measurement

Campaign ledger: empty on purpose, with fixed columns

The campaign ledger starts with zero rows and defined columns. Every row will come from an authorized first-party report with the date it was read. No estimates.

There are zero rows because the first report has not been read yet. That is the whole of what is known about this site's performance.

The ledger is empty

Columns are defined. No row has been entered, because no first-party report has been read yet. Nothing here is estimated.

The columns

  • timestamp — ISO 8601 UTC instant at which the row was recorded.
  • source — Where the numbers came from, naming the exact report — for example "Associates Click Reports" or "Associates Orders Report".
  • channel — Where the traffic was referred from. Must be a channel declared in the Associates account (S02).
  • eligible_sessions — Sessions as Amazon defines them: the session begins at the click-through and ends at 24 hours, an order, or another Special Link click (S02).
  • clicks — Outbound clicks reported by the Associates Click Reports for that channel and period.
  • reported_orders — Orders reported in the Associates Orders Report. Not inferred from clicks.
  • provenance — The document, export filename or screenshot the row was transcribed from, plus who read it.

Frozen rules

  1. Never enter an estimated or modelled conversion rate.
  2. Never reconcile a row that mixes an Associates figure with a site-side figure in one column.
  3. Never backfill a date you did not actually read the report on.

Why it starts empty

A conversion rate needs a numerator and a denominator drawn from the same population. The denominator would have to come from an analytics source; the numerator only ever arrives in the Associates Orders Report. They do not count the same things. Until both exist, the only honest entry is a blank. S02

What will fill it

  1. Verify the site in Search Console — see Q-07.
  2. Choose a reporting cadence and stick to it; a weekly read beats a daily glance.
  3. Transcribe one row per report, with the export filename and the date you read it.
