Suggestions, and the decision each one waits on

Kitchen Counter · Register updated 2026-11-19 · Nothing here is implemented by being listed

This is the review desk. Every proposal carries its problem, the evidence behind it, the options considered, a recommendation, and the person who decides. Machine-readable copy: data/suggestions.json. The questions that block these proposals live on Open questions; what was actually decided is on the decision log.

How to read the status. Implemented means the change is live on the site today. Partly implemented means part of the change is live and the remainder is tracked as an open question or flag — the row says which. Proposed means it is waiting for a decision and has no effect yet. Nothing in the proposed column has been quietly half-built.

SG-06 · owner decides

Buy one air fryer and one thermometer, and earn the right to say "we used it"

Problem. The highest-value content in this niche — what the thing is like to live with — cannot be written honestly without the thing.

Evidence. FTC: "You can't talk about your experience with a product you haven't tried" (S03). Google's E-E-A-T guidance rewards demonstrated experience (S21).

Options.

  • A. Buy one unit per family out of the commission budget and document a fixed set of tests: heat-up time, evenness with a thermocouple grid, basket clean-up time, counter footprint.
  • B. Stay specification-only, and be the site that is scrupulous about it.
  • C. Accept review units only if the site discloses the gift.

Recommendation. B until the site earns anything, then A. A is the only durable moat in this niche, but it should be funded by the site, not by a credit card.

SG-13 · owner decides

Test one short link inside the enrolled Associates workflow before trusting the format

Problem. The five supplied links use link.amazon, and their status in the enrolled workflow cannot be settled from outside the account.

Evidence. Program Policies 2(b) prohibits a shortener used so the destination is unclear (S02). The site labels every product button as going to Amazon and carries rel="sponsored", so the destination is not hidden from the reader; but the supplied short links' own status in the enrolled workflow is unresolved (F-13), and the site claims neither compliance nor failure.

Options.

  • A. Click one link while signed in, confirm the destination ASIN and confirm the click appears in the Associates report, then repeat for the rest.
  • B. Switch every link to direct /dp/ URLs and drop the short form.
  • C. Declare the short links non-compliant and remove them.

Recommendation. A for the short form as provenance, with the site continuing to publish direct URLs either way. C is a claim the evidence does not support.

SG-08 · engineering decides

Add a working outbound-click counter that stores nothing about the reader

Problem. There is no way to tell whether a guide is read or merely visited.

Evidence. Program Policies 2(b) forbids tying a sub-tag or ID to an individual user (S02). So the counter must be aggregate by construction, not by policy.

Options.

  • A. Client-side counter: one integer per product in localStorage, no identifier, no network call. Honest but per-browser.
  • B. A self-hosted endpoint that logs a path and a product id and discards the IP.
  • C. A third-party analytics script.

Recommendation. A is shipped because it touches nothing and cannot leak. B is the upgrade once there is a host to run it on — it is the only option that gives a real denominator. C is the option that turns a small site into a privacy policy.

How a suggestion becomes a change

  1. It appears here with an ID, a problem, and a recommendation.
  2. An owner decision is recorded in the decision log with a date and a source.
  3. The change is made, and the status here moves to implemented on the same day.
  4. If the decision was to do nothing, it is recorded as rejected with the reason. Silence is not a decision.
