Chefman 6 Qt Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven

Kitchen Counter · Listing snapshot 2026-11-19 · Destination re-verified 2026-11-20 · Seller statements, not test results

This is the site's starting pick for air fryers, for one reason: the layout. The listing describes two independent 3-quart baskets with a Sync Finish control, so two foods can run at two settings and finish at the same time. That is the most flexible layout of the three air fryers on this site. It is the wrong shape if you want to bake or roast with oven-style space — that is the Emeril's job below — or if you would rather wash one deep basket than two shallow ones.

Where the listing disagrees with itself. The listing's Capacity field reads “6 liters” while its title and bullets read “6-quart” with two 3-quart baskets. Six US quarts is about 5.68 liters, so the field is not a conversion of the title. Both readings stay on the record; neither is corrected by us — the conflict is logged in data/products.json (P-01) and summarized on Flagged for Review.

Who this one fits

Consider it if you regularly cook two different foods at once — fries for one person and chicken for another — and want them to finish together without sharing a pan. The 13.38-inch depth is the middle of this set: measure your counter before ordering, and remember the listing does not state the clearance a running unit needs.

Look elsewhere if you want oven-style baking with a wide low-temperature range (the Emeril publishes 75–500°F), or one large basket instead of two small ones (the 8-quart TurboFry).
