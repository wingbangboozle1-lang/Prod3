Direction · Suggestions register

Suggestions, and the decision each one waits on

Kitchen Counter · Register updated 2026-11-20 · Nothing here is implemented by being listed

This is the review desk. Every proposal carries its problem, the evidence behind it, the options considered, a recommendation, and the person who decides. Machine-readable copy: data/suggestions.json. The questions that block these proposals live on Open questions; what was actually decided is on the decision log.

How to use this page. Read a card, then mark it. The block at the bottom collects your marks into text you can paste back into a session or a document. Implemented means it is live today; retired means it was decided against and the row keeps the reason; proposed means it has no effect until someone chooses.
How to read the status. Implemented means the change is live on the site today. Partly implemented means part of the change is live and the remainder is tracked as an open question or flag — the row says which. Proposed means it is waiting for a decision and has no effect yet. Nothing in the proposed column has been quietly half-built.
