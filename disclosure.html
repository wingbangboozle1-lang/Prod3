How we disclose

Kitchen Counter · Reviewed against the Operating Agreement and FTC guidance, 2026-11-19

The short version

The required statement “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” appears above the fold and in the footer of every page, with conditional wording: if an eligible qualifying purchase is credited, Kitchen Counter may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The site owner supplied tracking ID shopnow047c-20. We have not verified that this ID is assigned to an enrolled Associates account, that the account is eligible, or that the supplied short links meet the program's current requirements — see FLG-006, FLG-010, FLG-011. We do not claim the short links are invalid.

What remains unverified

  • The owner-supplied tracking ID's assignment to an active account and that account's eligibility.
  • Whether each supplied short link is a compliant Special Link and records the intended product. The observed redirect and the owner's intended product are kept in separate fields.
  • Commission, conversion, or revenue results. The performance ledger is empty (FLG-015).
  • Price and availability data: not displayed anywhere, so nothing is claimed about them beyond pointing you to the live listing (FLG-007).

Where the disclosure appears

  • Above the fold on every page — a strip directly under the masthead, before any product content.
  • Repeated in the footer of every page.
  • No prices at all — Amazon's Program Policies (section 2(b)) permit displaying price and availability only when Amazon serves that data or it arrives through the Product Advertising API. We do neither, so no price appears anywhere on this site; you confirm it on the listing.

This follows the FTC's guidance that disclosures be placed so they are hard to miss, with the endorsement itself, in simple clear language. Source: FTC Disclosures 101.

How product links are marked

Every outbound product link carries rel="sponsored", per Google's guidance for paid links. Source: Google Search Central — Qualify outbound links.

What we do not do

  • We do not claim a product is "trending," "best-selling," or "popular" unless the listing itself shows a dated, checkable rank — and then we quote it with its source.
  • We do not use product photography we did not license. Per the Operating Agreement, Program Content (data, images, text) is provided by Amazon; we use CSS/SVG art instead of copied photos. Source: Operating Agreement §1.
  • We do not display prices in any form. Earlier versions of this site showed dated prices; they were removed on 2026-11-19 and a release check now fails if a currency amount reappears.
