Direction: decisions, the path chooser, and where the registers live

Kitchen Counter · Updated 2026-11-20 · Decisions recorded here; proposals live in the suggestions register

This page is where the site's next moves are chosen. Each suggestion carries a status: implemented means it shipped and you can inspect it; proposed means it waits for a path. Each open question carries the concrete action that would answer it — and who can take that action. Nothing here is a claim about popularity, traffic, or product superiority.

Current status (registers last generated 2026-11-19): the front page leads with product cards, chooser, seasonal guides, and comparison data. SG-17 keeps price display off unless compliant Amazon-served or API data is configured. F-21 / Q-21 concern whether the shipped 1200×630 PNG social-preview image renders in external share debuggers; the former SVG format issue is resolved. The complete suggestion list is in the Suggestions register.
