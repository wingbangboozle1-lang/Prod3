Which fits your cooking?

Grill and check often? The ThermoMaven: one second and ±0.5°F, at a fraction of the wireless set's price. Long smokes and roasts where you walk away? The CHEF iQ Sense: three probes, phone alerts, flip and rest timers — at a much higher price. Neither is "better"; they answer different questions.
