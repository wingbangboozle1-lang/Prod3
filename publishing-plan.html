What we plan to publish next

Kitchen Counter · Plan dated 2026-11-20 · Nothing on this page is a published guide or a promise about traffic

This is a proposal list, not a schedule of claims. Each row names a question a shopper actually asks, the week it is proposed for, and the evidence it needs before it can be written. Four rows are already blocked on a figure no official source publishes, and they say so instead of guessing. When Search Console is live, this order gets re-chosen from what people actually search — until then it is an editorial hypothesis and is labelled as one.

What this page will not do. No demand figure, no traffic forecast, no sales estimate, no review count, no price and no “best” verdict appears here or in any proposed row. A proposal becomes a guide when its evidence exists, not when it is written.

How this list gets re-ordered

The decisions behind it →
Today

Editorial judgement, stated as a hypothesis

The order follows the calendar and what a shopper plausibly asks at that moment. The site has no traffic data (FLG-015) and no Search Console property yet, so any claim that these are the most-wanted topics would be invented. This page does not make that claim.

After Search Console

Measured queries, re-chosen monthly

Once the property is verified and the sitemap is submitted, the next topic is chosen from impressions and queries. A row that earns no impressions in a month is demoted, not quietly rewritten — and the demotion is recorded here rather than deleted.

Reading the two registers together

Ideas & review holds proposals about the site — navigation, capture, measurement, compliance. This page holds proposals about what to write next. Both are review surfaces: a row exists to be accepted, changed or rejected before anything is built or published.

Plan data: data/editorial-plan.json. The page is checked against that file by the release gate, so a row cannot be edited in one place only.
