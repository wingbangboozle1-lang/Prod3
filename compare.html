Pick by the number that constrains you, not by a winner

Counter space is the limit

The shallowest unit is the Chefman 8 Qt at 12.75 in deep; the Emeril oven is 16.9 in deep and 14.8 in tall. Measure the spot before comparing anything else.

You cook two things at once

Only the Chefman 6 Qt dual lists two separate 3-qt baskets with Sync Finish. One 3-qt zone is smaller than the 8 qt single basket, so “more baskets” is not “more food”.

You want to stop guessing at doneness

Handheld = one probe, read on the spot (ThermoMaven). Wireless = three probes you leave in and read in an app (CHEF iQ Sense). Both accuracy figures are seller claims.

How to use the dimensions

Listed exterior dimensions help screen for fit; they do not state the clearance needed around a running appliance or its usable cooking area. Measure the actual counter and check the maker's instructions for required clearance. Quart labels are not serving-size claims; the Chefman dual-basket listing specifies two separate 3-qt baskets.
