Air fryer oven vs toaster oven: the footprint test

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19 · 5 min read · Specs from official listings

An air fryer oven and a toaster oven occupy the same counter. The difference is what happens inside: convection air-frying plus baking, broiling, dehydrating, and roasting in one cavity. The Emeril Lagasse 26QT is our verified example — here is the footprint test to run on any of them.

Run the footprint test

Emeril: 16.9"D × 15.8"W × 14.8"H. A typical toaster oven is shallower. The extra depth buys a 26-quart cavity that fits a 6-lb chicken and a 12-inch pizza (listing). If your counter is 24 inches deep with a backsplash and outlets, 16.9 inches leaves little clearance — measure first.

Count the functions you will use

The Emeril lists 24 presets (title) / 25 (About) — flagged conflict. Functions include air fry, bake, broil, dehydrate, roast, slow cook, and toast with auto shut-off. If you will use three, a basket fryer is cheaper and smaller. If you will use six, the oven replaces six appliances.

Weigh the parts

Six accessories (rack, bake pan, drip tray, crisper basket, rotisserie spit, fetch tool) mean more washing than a single basket. The trade is versatility for cleanup — see cleanup and counter-space trade-offs.

The footprint test in one line: depth × width × functions-you-will-use. If any of the three fails, the toaster oven or basket fryer is the better buy.
Does an air fryer oven toast as well as a toaster oven?

The Emeril listing describes a toast countdown with auto shut-off and an indicator light. We quote the listing; we do not test toast.
