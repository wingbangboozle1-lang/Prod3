Best for

A single layer of fries, wings or vegetables, and anyone who wants the simplest machine that still has a high-heat mode.

Not for

Cooking two components at once, or anything tall.

What the listing says

  • Single basket, 8 qt, with a 450 °F Hi-Fry option and four presets for fries, chicken, meat and fish.
  • Modes listed: air fry, bake, dehydrate and frozen. Automatic shut-off, an LED shake reminder and programmable control.
  • 12.75 in deep by 13.5 in wide by 10 in tall; stainless steel, with a black variant also listed.
  • At capture the listing showed 4.4 out of 5 stars from 16,890 ratings and an Amazon's Choice badge.

Where it is weak

It is a multi-size listing — the same ASIN family offers 6 qt and 9 qt — so confirm which size you are buying. And 10 in tall is shallow for 8 qt, which is good for a single layer and bad for anything standing up.

The fit test

If you want one basket and a high-heat finish, this fits. If you regularly cook two things at different temperatures, a single basket will cost you a second batch every night.

Quick read

  • One basket, 8 qt, 12.75 in deep — the simplest and shallowest of the three air fryers here.
  • Dehydrate is a real mode, not a preset label, so it can do jerky and dried fruit as well as frying.
  • A shake reminder and a 450 F Hi-Fry option cover the two things single-basket cooking usually gets wrong: uneven crisping and a ceiling too low to finish food.

Watch out for

  • The basket is one compartment. Two foods need either two batches or one mixed batch that cooks at the same rate.
  • 8 qt is generous but shallow: 10 in tall, so a tall roast will not fit even though the volume looks large.
  • This ASIN is a multi-size listing. Confirm the size you receive; the link resolves to the 8 qt variant.
