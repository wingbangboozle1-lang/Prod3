The questions that have to be answered before the next decision

Kitchen Counter · Register updated 2026-11-20 · 27 open questions, grouped by what they block

A decision made on missing information is a guess with better manners. Each row states what is known, what is not, why it matters, and the cheapest concrete action that would settle it. Machine-readable copy: data/open-questions.json.

Read this first. Several rows can only be answered from inside the Associates account — they are marked owner-only. They are not blockers for publishing; they are blockers for claiming that the site earns anything. Nothing on this site presents a conversion rate, revenue figure, or ranking, because the data behind those figures does not exist yet.
