Tools

Three things to do before you choose

Kitchen Counter · Listings read 2026-11-20 · Arithmetic, not testing

None of these tools predicts how an appliance will perform. Two of them do arithmetic on numbers the makers publish, and the third reproduces a government food-safety table without editorialising it. That is the honest limit of what a site that has not tested anything can offer.

Compare

Build your own comparison

Tick only the specs you care about, and only the products you are actually weighing.
Convert

Oven-to-air-fryer converter

A starting temperature from an oven recipe, with the convention named and the caveat attached.
Cook safely

Cook-to-temperature card

USDA FSIS safe minimum internal temperatures, with rest times, searchable and printable.

Why these three

They answer the three questions buyers actually get stuck on: which numbers matter to me, how do I use this thing for the food I already cook, and when is it actually done. A buying guide can describe those; a tool can answer them for your specific case in about fifteen seconds.

What these tools will not do. They will not rank the five products, produce a price, or tell you which one cooks better. Nothing here has been tested, and a calculator is not a substitute for a measurement.
