About the desk, and how this site is written

Who writes this site, the five rules it follows, how the affiliate disclosure is placed, and what it deliberately does not do — including testing products it has not handled.

This site is written by a small editorial desk. It does not have a personality, a founder with a favourite pan, or a testing lab. What it has is the patience to read five specification sheets closely and the discipline to say so when something is missing.

The method

  1. Every product gets its own listing read, on one date, and every value is copied out with a source.
  2. If a value is absent, it stays absent. A blank with a flag is published instead of a guess.
  3. If a listing contradicts itself, both readings are published and the conflict is named.
  4. Food-safety figures come from the USDA, not from a summary of the USDA.
  5. No price is stored or shown, because Amazon's Program Policies restrict who may display price and availability.

All five sources are listed on the source register, and every unresolved issue is on the Flagged for review page.

How this site discloses

The sentence required by the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement appears in a strip beneath the masthead on every page, above the first heading, and again in the footer. The same requirement is repeated next to every outbound product button. The strip is deliberately annoying, because a disclosure that is easy to miss is not a disclosure.

Every outbound link to Amazon is marked rel="sponsored noopener", is labelled as going to Amazon, and opens in a new tab. This site does not use a link shortener for anything it publishes: the destination is always a full amazon.com address so nobody has to guess where a click goes.

What this site is not

  • It is not affiliated with, owned by, or endorsed by Amazon.com, Inc.
  • It is not a testing lab. No unit of any product here has been handled.
  • It does not publish prices, availability or delivery estimates.
  • It does not rank products as "best" — it explains which one fits a given cook and counter.

Who to talk to

Corrections are the point of the exercise. If a figure here disagrees with the listing, the listing is right and this page is a bug — see the suggestions board and the open questions for what is already known to be unsettled.
