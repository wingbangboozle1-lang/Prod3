CHEF iQ Sense — Smart Wireless Meat Thermometer (3 Probes, WiFi)

Kitchen Counter · Listing snapshot 2026-11-19 · Destination re-verified 2026-11-20 · Seller statements, not test results

The leave-in system. Three ultra-thin probes stay in the food and report through a Smart Hub to your phone over WiFi and Bluetooth, so a long cook — a brisket, a smoke, a Sunday roast — can be watched without opening the grill. Two things to accept deliberately: the readings live in an app (the listing's own display-type field says “App”), and the listing tells two different stories about battery life and warranty, both shown below. This record is the 3-probe variant of a multi-variant listing that also sells 1, 2, and 4-probe sets.

Who this one fits

Consider it if you cook long or leave-alone — grills, smokers, roasts — and want continuous probe readings on your phone instead of opening the lid to spot-check. Three probes means three cuts or two cuts plus the ambient sensor doing different jobs at once.

Look elsewhere if you do quick weeknight checks and do not want an app in the loop — a handheld like the ThermoMaven is the simpler habit — or if the unresolved battery and warranty numbers bother you more than they bother us.
