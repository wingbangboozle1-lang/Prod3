Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Air Fryer Toaster Oven

Kitchen Counter · Listing snapshot 2026-11-19 · Destination re-verified 2026-11-20 · Seller statements, not test results

This is the oven-shaped option. The listing describes a 26-quart countertop cavity with French doors, and its published range runs 75–500°F — the widest here, and the only one that goes below 200°F. If you want one machine that claims to bake, roast, and crisp, this is the shape. The trade is depth: at 16.9 inches listed, it is the deepest unit in this set, and it is the one where measuring your counter first is not optional.

Who this one fits

Consider it if you want oven-style baking and rotisserie space, a published range that reaches both 75°F and 500°F, and you have at least 16.9 inches of depth plus the clearance the manual requires. It is also the only one here that names a rotisserie spit and fetch tool in the box.

Look elsewhere if your counter is tight, or if you only ever cook one or two things at a time — a 26-quart cavity you use like a 6-quart basket is space you gave up for nothing.
