Where it is weak

The preset count disagrees inside the same listing — 24 in the title, 25 in a bullet. Also: no star rating was present on the page at capture, so this site publishes none.

The fit test

Measure the depth of your counter and the swing of the nearest cabinet. If either fails, this machine fails, regardless of how good the capacity looks.

Quick read

  • 26 qt is a different class of machine from a 6 qt basket fryer; it is a countertop oven that also air fries.
  • French doors open outward instead of a drop-down door, which changes how much clearance you need in front of it and how you reach in.
  • The listing states it fits a 12 in pizza and rotisseries a 6 lb chicken — two concrete tests that a basket fryer at this price will not pass.

Watch out for

  • The preset count differs inside the same listing: 24 in the title, 25 in a bullet. It is not published here as a spec (F-02).
  • 26 qt in the title against 24.6 liters in the field: 26 US quarts is about 24.6 litres, so these two do agree. Unlike P1, there is no unit conflict here.
  • 17 in of counter depth is roughly half a metre. That is the largest footprint of the five products and the first thing to check.
