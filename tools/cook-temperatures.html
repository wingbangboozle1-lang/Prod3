When it is actually done

Kitchen Counter · Values transcribed from the USDA FSIS chart, last updated 14 April 2025 · Source S17

This is the one page on the site where every number is official rather than seller-supplied. The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service publishes minimum internal temperatures, measured with a food thermometer, and the rest times are part of the rule — not a suggestion. The table below reproduces that chart.

Reading the table correctly

  • Internal, not ambient. These temperatures describe the centre of the food as measured with a food thermometer — not the oven dial, and not the surface.
  • Rest time is part of the rule. For steaks, chops, roasts and fresh ham the 3-minute rest is required alongside the 145°F reading. The rest continues the cooking and lets the temperature distribute.
  • Higher is allowed. The USDA states that consumers may choose to cook to higher temperatures for personal preference. Nothing here is a ceiling.
  • Placement matters. The reading is only as good as where the probe is. The USDA publishes separate guidance on probe placement, registered as S18 in the source register and discussed in the accuracy guide.
Why this page exists on a shopping site. Because a thermometer is only useful if you know what number you are looking for. The two thermometers on this site are compared on their seller-claimed accuracy; this table is the target those claims are supposed to hit.
