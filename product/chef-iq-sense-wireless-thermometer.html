Best for

Smoking, long roasts and any cook where you want to watch the curve rather than take a single reading.

Not for

Anyone who wants a thermometer that works without a phone, a network and an app.

What the listing says

  • Three ultra-thin wireless probes with four internal sensors plus one ambient sensor.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, readings in the CHEF iQ app, up to 50 hours on a charge.
  • Probes described as heat-safe to 1000 °F, waterproof and dishwasher safe.
  • A two-year assurance from CHEF iQ, stated in a bullet. At capture the listing showed 4.2 out of 5 stars from 2,178 ratings.

Where it is weak

The specification field lists around twenty standards including FDA, ISO 13485 and HACCP. That is a seller-populated attribute, not a certificate this site has seen. Physical dimensions are not published either.

The fit test

If the appeal of a thermometer is 'I do not have to stand there', this is the product. If the appeal is 'I want to know right now', the instant-read is cheaper and simpler.

Quick read

  • Three probes means three different proteins can be tracked to three different targets in the same cook.
  • The ambient sensor reads oven or grill temperature alongside the food, which is the difference between 'cook 20 minutes' and 'cook until the food is at target'.
  • App-based readings with a stated unlimited range over Wi-Fi are what you want if the smoker is outside and you are not.
  • A stated two-year assurance and dishwasher-safe probes lower the running cost of owning it.

Watch out for

  • It is app-dependent. The listing's display type is 'App', so a phone and a Wi-Fi network are part of the product, not accessories.
  • The specification field lists about twenty certification schemes; that is a seller-populated attribute and not a substitute for a certificate you can read (F-06).
  • Probe length and hub size are not published on the listing (F-04).
  • This is the priciest class of product in the set; the extra cost buys connectivity and probes, not accuracy versus a good instant-read.
