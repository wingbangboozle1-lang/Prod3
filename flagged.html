FLG-007 — Prices are not displayed. Amazon’s Program Policies permit showing price and availability only when Amazon serves the data or it arrives through the Product Advertising API. Basis: Operating Agreement (S01) · Program Policies (S02)

Kitchen Counter has not tested the unit or examined the certificate; NIST certifies traceability only of measurement results it provides, not retail products. Both readings stay visible; no accuracy value is presented as measured. Data: data/products.json P-04 claim-marked specs. Explainer: What ±0.5°F means.

Basis: NIST traceability FAQ (S16)

Decision needed: owner confirms account status and updates disclosure.html accordingly.

Basis: Operating Agreement (S01) · Program Policies (S02)

FLG-011 — The /dp/<token> Page-Not-Found check: investigated and dismissed as link evidence. On 2026-11-19, pasting each 9-character token into amazon.com/dp/<token> returned Page Not Found. Dismissal rationale: a /dp/ URL constructed from a short-link token is not the supplied Special Link; the result neither validates nor invalidates the short links, and repeating it as a compliance conclusion would be wrong. The 9-character length is the only supported finding (tokens are not ASINs). Recorded in data/sources.json S15 as an observation.

Basis: Operating Agreement (S01) · Program Policies (S02)
