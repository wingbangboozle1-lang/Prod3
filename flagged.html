Flagged for review: open irregularities

Kitchen Counter · Audit update 2026-11-19 · These items do not block the site — most need owner decisions

FLG-001 — Intended product for each short link is unconfirmed. All five submitted tokens are 9 characters, not ASINs (an ASIN is 10 characters). The observed redirect destinations (B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z) are recorded separately from the blank intended-ASIN fields and are not treated as confirmation of intent. Decision needed: owner confirms intended ASIN per link in the Associates workflow. Data: data/products.json (intended_*/observed_* fields).
FLG-002 — Emeril preset count contradicts itself. Title says 24 presets; "About this item" says 25. Both are published; neither is chosen. Source: B09B7SB46R.
FLG-003 — CHEF iQ battery life and warranty contradict themselves. Battery: 50 hours (About this item) vs 70 hours (Product Description). Warranty: 2-year assurance (About) vs 3 Year (Item details). Both published; neither chosen. Source: B0DZFD6R9Z.
FLG-004 — Chefman 8 Qt has a newer model. The listing page links to B0CNY1YVDD (Chefman 6 QT Compact) as "a newer model of this item." Decide whether to migrate the pick. Source: B08DKYBTPH.
FLG-005 — ThermoMaven has a newer model. The listing page links to B0GL7Z4KP4 (orange variant) as "a newer model of this item." Decide whether to migrate the pick. Source: B0DHZTCNR5.
FLG-006 — Short-link compliance status is unresolved. The supplied link.amazon links are used as-is. Their exact compliance status under the Operating Agreement is unresolved — we do not claim they are invalid. Decision needed: test one Special Link per product in the enrolled Associates workflow and confirm destination and tracking report before scaling. References: Operating Agreement · Program Policies.
FLG-007 — Prices drift. All prices are archival snapshots dated 2026-11-19 and will change. Every price on the site is dated; always verify the current price, availability, and seller on Amazon before purchase.
FLG-009 — ThermoMaven accuracy/NIST/NSF wording is seller marketing, not a test. ±0.5°F, “NIST & NSF Certified,” the 1-second read, IP67, and the included calibration certificate are listing claims. Kitchen Counter has not tested the unit or examined the certificate; NIST certifies traceability only of measurement results it provides, not retail products. Both readings stay visible; no accuracy value is presented as measured. Data: data/products.json P-04 claim-marked specs. Explainer: What ±0.5°F means.
FLG-010 — Amazon Associates account status is unverified. Tracking ID shopnow047c-20 was supplied by the site owner, but its assignment to an enrolled account and that account’s eligibility have not been verified. Participation statements were removed from the site; disclosure copy is conditional (“if a qualifying purchase is credited”). Decision needed: owner confirms account status and updates disclosure.html accordingly.
FLG-011 — The /dp/<token> Page-Not-Found check: investigated and dismissed as link evidence. On 2026-11-19, pasting each 9-character token into amazon.com/dp/<token> returned Page Not Found. Dismissal rationale: a /dp/ URL constructed from a short-link token is not the supplied Special Link; the result neither validates nor invalidates the short links, and repeating it as a compliance conclusion would be wrong. The 9-character length is the only supported finding (tokens are not ASINs). Recorded in data/sources.json S15 as an observation.
FLG-012 — Price/rating snapshot metadata is incomplete. Snapshots record the date 2026-11-19 only — not the observation time, delivery location, seller, or coupon state. Prices and ratings therefore cannot be treated as reproducible observations or current offers. Data: data/products.json price_observed / rating fields.
FLG-013 — Emeril accessories count contradicts itself. The listing title says “Six Cooking Accessories Included” and the About section names six, but the configuration field says “7 Accessories.” All readings stay visible; none is chosen. Source: B09B7SB46R · Data: data/products.json P-02.
FLG-014 — Reader email signup is not connected. The demo signup form (which showed a fake confirmation) was removed in the 2026-11-19 audit. No email provider, endpoint, or consent flow exists; reader_signup is a reserved event that fires only on a confirmed provider response. Subscription path today: RSS. Data: assets/app.js · Analytics & privacy.
FLG-015 — Campaign ledger is empty by design. No clicks, orders, commissions, or revenue have been first-party reported. The ledger accepts rows only from authorized first-party reports; no conversion score or revenue forecast is presented anywhere. Data: data/campaign-ledger.json.
FLG-008 — No product photography. The site uses CSS/SVG art, not product photos. Per Operating Agreement §1, Program Content includes images provided by Amazon; using only Amazon-provided images (or none) is the compliant path. Decide whether to license Amazon-provided images.
