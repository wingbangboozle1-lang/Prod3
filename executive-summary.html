Kitchen Counter: listed specs, plainly told

Kitchen Counter · 2026-11-19 · Tracking ID shopnow047c-20

What it is

A publication-style Amazon affiliate site covering two kitchen categories — air fryers (3 products) and meat thermometers (2 products). The front page leads with products, not process; every dashboard surface is reachable from the footer and one navigation group. Eight question-shaped guides, a comparison table, a buying guide, and a 3-question chooser help visitors decide.

What is sourced — and what is not (audit 2026-11-19)

  • Specs and ratings are transcribed from dated official listing snapshots and cited to them. They are seller statements, not independent Kitchen Counter tests. No price appears anywhere on the site (Program Policies §2(b)); any price observations live solely in internal data files with date-only metadata (FLG-007/012).
  • The five supplied link.amazon links were recorded as supplied. During the audit they resolved to listings B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH (Air Fryers) and B0DHZTCNR5, B0DZFD6R9Z (Meat Thermometers); observed destinations are kept separate from blank intended-ASIN fields (FLG-001).
  • Policy and guidance pages cited: Amazon Operating Agreement and Program Policies, FTC Disclosures 101, Google rel="sponsored", GitHub Pages docs, plus NIST traceability and USDA FSIS temperature/placement guidance.
  • Disclosure strip above the fold on every page with the required “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” statement plus conditional commission wording; rel="sponsored" on every Amazon link (FLG-010 documents that account status is unverified).
  • The demo email signup with a fake confirmation was removed; measurement is local-count only with no endpoint (FLG-014, Analytics & privacy).

What is flagged (does not block)

  • FLG-001: intended ASIN per short link unconfirmed (owner confirmation needed).
  • FLG-002/003/013: three listing-internal conflicts (preset count; battery life + warranty; accessories count).
  • FLG-004/005: two listings cross-promote other models.
  • FLG-006: short-link compliance unresolved — test one Special Link per product before scaling. FLG-011: the /dp/<token> Page-Not-Found check was investigated and dismissed as link evidence.
  • FLG-007/012: prices drift; snapshot metadata is date-only.
  • FLG-008: no product photography used (compliant path).
  • FLG-009: ThermoMaven accuracy/NIST wording is seller claim, not test.
  • FLG-010: Associates account status unverified.
  • FLG-014: email signup not connected. FLG-015: campaign ledger empty by design.
  • FLG-016: SVG social-preview image unverified in share previewers (F-21).
